Controls

Rotate the cue by dragging on the felt. The dark wheel on the right offers fine aiming. Pull the power bar on the left downward, then release to strike. Tap the cue ball during ball-in-hand to reposition it. Desktop: ←/→ aim, ↑/↓ power, Space to shoot.

Internet two-player • peer-to-peer (experimental)

Two browsers can connect directly with a manual offer/answer. A STUN server is used for NAT discovery when available. This is not Bluetooth; browser Bluetooth cannot host the original Android Bluetooth Classic connection.
